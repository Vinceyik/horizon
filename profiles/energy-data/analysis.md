# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a natural gas item carries a usable number with a baseline, or an operational fact that changes supply, demand, or flow.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Balance-changing.** A storage, production, or export figure that materially changes the supply-demand balance against a named baseline, or an outage that removes meaningful volume.
- **7-8: Important.** Concrete data with a comparison: BCF versus consensus or five-year average, a price move with its starting level, LNG feedgas with a daily change.
- **5-6: Useful.** A single data point with context, a scheduled maintenance window, or a forecast with a named source and horizon.
- **3-4: Low value.** "Prices surged" with no figure, analyst opinion without data, or a figure without any baseline.
- **0-2: Noise.** Political commentary, undated speculation, or promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward BCF figures with their comparison period, prices with currency and units, percentage moves with the starting value, LNG volumes in Bcf/d, degree days with their reference period, and outages with capacity and duration. Never reward a large percentage move without the starting value. Distinguish EIA actuals from analyst estimates and from forecasts. Use three to five specific topic tags.
