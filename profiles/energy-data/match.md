# Natural gas data profile

Use this profile when an item carries a market figure, a balance change, or an operational fact about natural gas.

Typical items include US storage reports in BCF and their five-year comparison, Henry Hub, TTF, and JKM prices with percentage moves, LNG export volumes and terminal capacity, production and rig counts, heating and cooling degree days, pipeline maintenance and outages, and demand forecasts with a named source.

Do not route general energy-transition essays, political commentary without figures, or price commentary that only says prices rose or fell here.
