# Fashion and watches profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is a product, a collection, a collaboration, an auction result, or a design trend in watches, apparel, or jewellery.

Typical items include new model launches and specifications, brand collaborations, capsule collections, runway-to-retail drops, auction records and estimates, material or movement innovation, retail and pricing changes, and documented shifts in design direction.

Do not route celebrity styling, red-carpet galleries, shopping listicles, affiliate round-ups, resale hype without prices, or general lifestyle content here.
