# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a fashion or watch item carries concrete, useful information about a product, a price, a collaboration, an auction, or a design direction.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Defining.** A collection-defining launch, an auction record, or a brand-level strategic shift that changes what the category offers or costs.
- **7-8: Notable.** A release, collaboration, or auction result with concrete detail: model name, material, movement, price, date, or estimate.
- **5-6: Useful.** A documented trend or product detail that helps a reader understand a brand or the market, but with limited breadth.
- **3-4: Low value.** Press releases without specifications, celebrity styling, "best of" lists, affiliate round-ups, or resale hype without figures.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure marketing, undated rumours, or content with no product, price, or design substance.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward named products, materials, movement or craft details, prices with a currency, release dates, edition sizes, and auction estimates with a baseline. Treat a designer appointment as notable only when the source explains the expected change in direction. Distinguish reported facts from brand claims and unconfirmed leaks. Use three to five specific topic tags.
