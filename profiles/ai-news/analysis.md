# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an AI item tells a builder something they could act on: a capability change, an interface change, a cost change, or a verifiable result.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Structural.** A frontier model release with published capability or price changes, a major open-source release, or binding regulation with named obligations.
- **7-8: Important.** A concrete API, SDK, pricing, or tooling change; a reproducible benchmark improvement; a documented safety evaluation.
- **5-6: Useful.** A well-sourced technical write-up, a minor release, or a benchmark result with a clear baseline but limited consequence.
- **3-4: Low value.** Funding announcements without a product, opinion and prediction pieces, conference promotion, benchmark charts with no baseline.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure marketing, "AGI is coming" speculation, listicles, or content with no verifiable claim.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward named models and versions, benchmark scores with their benchmark name and comparison, API changes with before and after behaviour, pricing with units, and context lengths. Penalise a headline number without a baseline. Distinguish vendor claims from independent evaluation. Use three to five specific topic tags.
