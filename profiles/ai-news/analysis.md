# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an AI item changes what a solo builder using AI coding tools can actually do this week — not whether it advances the research frontier.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Immediately usable.** A new model or tool, or a major update to one, that a solo builder can try today, with visible improvements to real work and clear access: free tier, price, signup link.
- **7-8: Useful soon.** A concrete feature, integration, or pricing change that alters daily workflows: better editor or agent support, longer context, cheaper API, a polished open-source tool with a demo.
- **5-6: Worth knowing.** Indirect but relevant: a comparison of tools a builder might pick, a policy change with mild impact, a well-explained technique.
- **3-4: Low value.** Deep infrastructure work (kernels, compilers, training recipes, distributed systems), paper-only results, funding news without a product, conference promotion.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure marketing, "AGI is coming" opinion, listicles, or anything with no product, price, or usable change.

# Evaluation guidance

Ask one question: could a person who builds with AI assistants, but does not train models, use this within a week? Reward free tiers, availability in Hong Kong and Asia, editor integrations, and concrete examples of what can be built. Penalise research-internal detail unless the item states the practical consequence in one sentence. Distinguish vendor claims from independent results. Use three to five specific topic tags.
